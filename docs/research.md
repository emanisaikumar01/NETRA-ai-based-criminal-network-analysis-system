# NETRA Research

## 1. Problem Statement

Investigative agencies often need to analyze information from multiple sources such as FIRs, call records, financial records, vehicle information, locations, criminal history, and historical cases.

When this information is stored separately, identifying hidden relationships and cross-case patterns requires significant manual effort.

NETRA aims to connect these heterogeneous data sources and provide investigators with explainable investigative leads.

---

## 2. Existing Approaches

Existing investigative systems can provide access to large collections of crime-related information.

However, challenges remain in:

- Connecting heterogeneous data sources
- Resolving duplicate or ambiguous entities
- Identifying hidden relationships
- Finding similarities between historical and current cases
- Explaining why a particular entity is considered relevant

---

## 3. NETRA's Approach

NETRA combines:

- Natural Language Processing (NLP)
- Entity Resolution
- Knowledge Graphs
- Network Analysis
- Anomaly Detection
- Cross-Case Similarity
- Explainable AI

---

## 4. Key Differentiator

NETRA focuses on converting fragmented information into connected, explainable investigative intelligence.

The system should show:

- Why a lead was generated
- Which evidence supports it
- What information is missing
- Confidence level of the lead

---

## 5. Evidence Gaps

NETRA distinguishes between:

- Available information
- Partial information
- Missing information

The system should not treat missing information as confirmed evidence.

---

## 6. Responsible AI

NETRA is designed to assist investigators and not automatically determine guilt or innocence.

Human investigators should review and validate all generated leads.

---

## 7. Privacy and Security

NETRA should include:

- Role-based access control
- Case-based authorization
- Audit logging
- Secure data handling
- Controlled access to sensitive information

---

## 8. Expected Benefits

- Faster investigation support
- Better relationship discovery
- Cross-case intelligence
- Explainable recommendations
- Improved evidence traceability
